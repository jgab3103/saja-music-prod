Open Guitar Chords

Guitar Foundations · Standard tuning (E–A–D–G–B–E) · Beginner

Learn the most useful open-position guitar chords. Choose a chord to see where your fingers go, then practise changing between chords using the progressions below.

Reading the diagrams: The six vertical lines are the guitar strings, with the thickest (low E) on the left. ● shows a finger position; ○ means play the open string; × means don't play that string. Finger numbers are 1 = index, 2 = middle, 3 = ring, 4 = little finger. F uses a small barre across the first two strings.
