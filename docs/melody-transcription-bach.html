Baroque Melodic Dictation Lab

Listen to a short passage, repeat it as often as you like, and transcribe the music onto manuscript paper. The opening beat gives you a foothold; the rest of the notation is concealed until you're ready to check.

1. Choose a passage

Loading music…

Choose the voice or voices you want to transcribe. You can listen to the full texture, just those voices, or the accompaniment.

2. Listen and transcribe

Preparing audio automatically…

Write the pitches and rhythms on manuscript paper. Use Play and Stop to isolate the passage, and leave Loop switched on to hear it repeatedly.

3. Your notation guide

The first quarter-note beat is shown in standard notation. The remaining notes are blurred until you reveal the answer. Each selected voice appears on its own staff. For a one-beat exercise, turn the guide off to hide the answer.

Listen, write your transcription, then reveal the score and compare it with your manuscript.

Scores are generated from MusicXML-derived repertoire data using VexFlow. Durations are measured in quarter-note beats. Audio is generated automatically in your browser; no upload or account is required.
