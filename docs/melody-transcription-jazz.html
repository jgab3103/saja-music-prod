SAJA MUSIC / AURAL SKILLS

Jazz Melodic Dictation Lab

Listen to improvised jazz phrases. Identify the notes in each phrase by ear—no rhythmic transcription required for this excercise, it is about hearing the nuances of jazz phrases. Phrases are drawn from a corpus of ten Keith Jarret jazz transcriptions. You will notice that he almost repeats the same phrase twice.

1. Choose a phrase

2. Listen and find the notes

Listen to the phrase, then press the piano keys for the pitches you hear. The first note is given. Your selected notes appear below the keyboard; rhythm is not assessed.
