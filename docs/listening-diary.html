Explore, compare, listen

Music history is not a single sequence of styles. Works written in the same decade can reveal very different musical worlds, shaped by local traditions, cultural exchange, travel, technology and performance. Narrow the dates, move around the map, and listen to contrasting works from the same period.

The map uses the place of composition recorded for each work; a first performance may have taken place somewhere else, and many works were composed over several years or in more than one location. Dates and places are a guide to exploration rather than a complete historical account. This is a growing, selective collection, not an exhaustive catalogue.
