Release notes

Public beta launch

3 November 2026

SAJA Music entered public beta! We're building an open collection of interactive music education resources for learners and teachers, including activities in rhythm, aural skills, piano, music theory and sound synthesis.

Beta means we're actively testing the experience across different devices and listening to feedback. We'll post notable additions and fixes here as they are released.

Known issues

These are areas we're investigating, not a complete list of problems. Behaviour may vary by device and browser.

Under investigation Older iPads: visual layout and controls

We are still investigating how best to manage backwards compatibility to older broswers and mobile devices. Note that it is likely this will be somewhere iOS 15 for Apple, but stay tuned for more something more definitive.

Under investigationAudio differences across devices

Audio playback and sound quality may have minor variation between browsers and devices. We are still finalising a standardised approach to manage mobile Audio. In the interim, audio playback should always work if your phone is not set to silent.

Feedback and discussion

We want SAJA Music to grow through conversations with the people who use it. Bug reports, teaching suggestions, requests for new exercises and thoughtful criticism are all welcome.
