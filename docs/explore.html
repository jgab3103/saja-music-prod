SAJA Music · At a glance

Music education, open to everyone.

SAJA Music develops freely accessible music education resources that bring together practical musicianship, learning design and interactive technology.

Our growing collection includes browser-based tools, structured exercises, articles and learning pathways for students, teachers and musicians. All resources are available without advertising, subscriptions or paywalls.

03 · Approach

How we develop resources

Music first

Resources are designed around musical activities: hearing, performing, reading, experimenting and creating. Interactive tools sit alongside written explanations and practical exercises.

Iterative development

SAJA Music is in beta. We are continuing to develop the collection, test resources across devices and revise material in response to use and feedback.
