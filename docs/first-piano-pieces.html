Times of Day — First Piano Pieces

Piano Foundations · Beginner · Eight pieces

Eight gentle pieces inspired by different times of day, for beginning pianists. Both hands remain in C position: right hand C4–G4, left hand C3–G3. The first two pieces use only low C semibreves in the left hand; later pieces introduce other bass notes. The left hand plays one semibreve per bar. The right hand plays four crotchets in each bar, finishing each piece with a C semibreve. The final three pieces introduce more stepwise melodic movement. Play gently, and listen to the sound of each note.

Original beginner compositions for SAJA Music. Requires an internet connection to load VexFlow.
